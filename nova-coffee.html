About URBAN MUG

URBAN MUG is a modern coffee brand concept created to deliver a premium and energetic visual identity.

The design focuses on strong typography, warm coffee aesthetics and a clean visual system that can work across packaging, social media and promotional materials.

The complete identity was designed to create a consistent and recognizable coffee brand experience.
